# Render and preview evidence

Use the contact sheet as an index, not as final visual proof. Open the cover, every QA-named frame, and any doubtful/risky cell at full size. Review the complete current revision; do not mix frames from different authored signatures.

For a declared reference, extract the matching source time with `ovs edit extract-frame` and compare it beside the target snapshot/draft frame. Pair frames by the manifest's spatial/temporal anchors, then judge the requested `reproduce`, `edit`, or `guide` relationship and its preserve/may-change boundary. Compare geometry, hierarchy, product identity, palette/type behavior, and timing/motion only where the sampled evidence supports them. Do not treat a probe or transcript as visual proof.

When the source and target differ, identify the exact frame/region and whether the difference is required by target copy, duration, language, canvas, or an approved simplification. Repair only concrete blockers. Keep supported deviations as preview/final-review notes instead of forcing an unrequested copy.
