# Ecommerce videos

Read only for product listing or sales creative after route lock. This reference supplements the selected route; a fully specified deterministic edit still goes directly to `stage-edit`.

Choose the commercial structure supported by evidence: demonstration, comparison, explanation, or promotion. Transfer a reference's hook/proof/CTA relationship to the user's product without copying unrelated claims. Anchor SKU, appearance, price/currency, offer conditions, and CTA in supplied facts; never invent results, testimonials, discounts, accessories, or operating behavior.

Route by needed control, not by “ecommerce”: cinematic product action may be direct generation; exact editable offer copy needs local layers. Reuse the actual product references and keep approved copy/tracks separate so a price or caption change reuses completed footage.

Apply `video-router/references/production-method.md` before review. Direct model output gets only the direct-delivery check. For local work, inspect representative candidate frames and compare them with the selected source, including locally authored labels and factual overlays. Confirm product identity, orientation, placement, readability, and non-obstruction. Judge rendered pixels rather than text extraction alone; report any unverified or mismatched attribute.
